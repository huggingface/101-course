# Join the Hugging Face Community

No w that you understand what the Hub is, let's shift gears in this course and start setting up you up for the chapters ahead.

## 1. Create a Hugging Face account

To create a Hugging Face account, go to [huggingface.co/join](https://huggingface.co/join).

> [!TIP]
> If you're part of a school, university, or company then use your organization email to easily find and join your friends and colleagues!

Once you're in, spend a few minutes setting up your profile. This isn't just vanity - it helps others understand who you are and what you're working on. Add a short bio, links to projects, and skills/interests so collaborators can find you.

## 2. Dive Into Community Discussions

Let's start by joining a conversation on a community board. [Here](https://huggingface.co/spaces/huggingface-101/README/discussions/1) you can find a conversation with other students. Drop a message to say hi!

## 3. Explore Spaces: AI in Action

Let's take things a bit further and try out a Space.

Spaces are interactive demos you can try in your browser without any installation or setup. We're going to explore and build these a lot in this course. For now, let's just browse through different categories to see what catches your eye. Here are some fun ones to get you started:

- [Web Scraper](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Agents-MCP-Hackathon/web-scraper) - a tool that can scrape websites and extract data  
- [Qwen Edit](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Qwen/Qwen-Image-Edit) - a tool that can edit images with AI.
- [Text to Speech Unlimited](https://huggingface.co/spaces/NihalGazi/Text-To-Speech-Unlimited) - a tool that can convert text to speech.

## 4. Join Organizations and Teams

Organizations on Hugging Face group work by companies, universities, labs, and communities. They help teams coordinate and share resources while contributing to the broader ecosystem.

Companies like Microsoft and Google share their latest research models and tools. Universities publish reproducible studies alongside student projects, making academic research more accessible. Research labs collaborate on breakthrough discoveries, sharing both successes and failed experiments. Indie groups build innovative projects together, often exploring creative applications. Enthusiast communities form around specific topics or technologies, and event organizers create spaces for conferences, workshops, and meetups.

> [!TIP]
> Check if your company or school has an org. You can also join orgs for tools or libraries you use. If not, you can always create your own!
