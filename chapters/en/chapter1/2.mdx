# Join the Hugging Face Community

Ready to join the AI community? The Hugging Face community is where developers, researchers, and practitioners share models, datasets, and ideas. Whether you're building a first demo or publishing research, you'll find tools and people to help.

<Tip>

New to AI? Start by exploring models on the Hub and ask questions in discussions.

</Tip>

## How the Community Works

Sharing drives progress. Someone uploads a model, others try it and suggest improvements, researchers share reproducible code, and the next iteration builds on it. It's open-source development applied to AI.

The community runs on mutual help. Experienced developers create tutorials, answer questions, and mentor newcomers. New users bring fresh perspectives and test models in unexpected ways. This creates a learning environment where everyone benefits.

<Tip>

Not sure how to contribute? Start small: test a model with your data, report issues, or improve unclear docs.

</Tip>

## Getting Started: Create Your Account

Create a Hugging Face account: [huggingface.co/join](https://huggingface.co/join).

<Tip>

If you're part of a school, university, or company then use your organization email to easily find and join your friends and colleagues!

</Tip>

Once you're in, spend a few minutes setting up your profile. This isn't just vanity - it helps others understand who you are and what you're working on.

Add a short bio, links to projects, and skills/interests so collaborators can find you.

<Tip>

Profile Pro Tips: Add a profile picture (people remember faces!), write a short bio about your interests, and mention what you're learning or building. This helps others find you for collaborations.

</Tip>

## Dive Into Community Discussions

Every model, dataset, and Space has a Community tab for focused discussions. These discussions serve as the primary venue for collaboration, troubleshooting, and knowledge sharing. You can ask questions about how to use a model effectively, report bugs or unexpected behavior, share your results and improvements with the community, or request new features and modifications.

Community discussions are intensely practical. People ask for model alternatives when their current choice isn't working, report issues with datasets, and share successful implementations.

<Tip>

Be specific in your questions, include code examples when relevant, and always say thanks when someone helps you out. The community thrives on mutual respect!

</Tip>

## Explore Spaces: AI in Action

Spaces are interactive demos you can try in your browser without any installation or setup. The variety of applications is remarkable. You'll find text generators for writing assistance, coding help, and creative brainstorming. Image creators and editors transform your descriptions into stunning visuals, while audio tools help with music generation, speech synthesis, and sound effect creation. Educational demos teach AI concepts through hands-on experimentation.

Browse through different categories to see what catches your eye. Each Space serves as a mini-tutorial on what's possible with AI, demonstrating practical applications and inspiring new ideas for your own projects.

<Tip>

Explore Spaces at [huggingface.co/spaces](https://huggingface.co/spaces). Examples:

- [Web Scraper](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Agents-MCP-Hackathon/web-scraper) - a tool that can scrape websites and extract data  
- [Qwen Edit](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Qwen/Qwen-Image-Edit) - a tool that can edit images with AI.
- [Text to Speech Unlimited](https://huggingface.co/spaces/NihalGazi/Text-To-Speech-Unlimited) - a tool that can convert text to speech.

You can find more live demos on the Spaces page.

</Tip>

## Join Organizations and Teams

Organizations on Hugging Face group work by companies, universities, labs, and communities. They help teams coordinate and share resources while contributing to the broader ecosystem.

Companies like Microsoft and Google share their latest research models and tools. Universities publish reproducible studies alongside student projects, making academic research more accessible. Research labs collaborate on breakthrough discoveries, sharing both successes and failed experiments. Indie groups build innovative projects together, often exploring creative applications. Enthusiast communities form around specific topics or technologies, and event organizers create spaces for conferences, workshops, and meetups.

<Tip>

Check if your company or school has an org. You can also join orgs for tools or libraries you use.

</Tip>

Organizations give you shared resources, team coordination tools, and access to collective expertise. Whether you're at a big tech company or a small startup, organizations help you work better together while contributing to the broader community.


## Ready to Jump In?

You're all set to join the Hugging Face community! Here's your action plan for getting started:

Start immediately by creating your account at [huggingface.co/join](https://huggingface.co/join). Set up your profile with a photo and short bio to help others understand who you are and what you're interested in. Browse a few models or Spaces that catch your attention to get a feel for what's available.

Within the first week, expand your engagement by joining the [Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/JfAtkvEtRb) and introducing yourself to the community. Ask a question in a model's Community tab - this is often the best way to get real-world advice. Follow some interesting creators and organizations to stay updated on new developments.

Over the first month, begin making more substantial contributions. Try building something with a model you discovered, even if it's simple. Share your experience in discussions, both successes and challenges. Make your first small contribution - even fixing a typo in documentation counts and helps you understand the contribution process.

<Tip>

Feeling overwhelmed? Start slow! Just create your account and browse around. The community is incredibly welcoming, and you can participate at your own pace. Every expert here started exactly where you are now.

</Tip>

The community is waiting to welcome you, learn from you, and build amazing things together. Your unique perspective matters, and your journey starts with a single click.
